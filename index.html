How it works

Get started in under a minute.

  1. Add your subjects and tasks with due dates and estimated hours.
  2. Check your risk level on the home screen and start with the red tasks.
  3. Use the focus timer and ask Immy for help when you are stuck.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Immy.

Is Immy free?

Yes, Immy is free for students.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open Immy in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app". It then works like a normal app.

What is the risk level?

Immy checks how many hours a task needs against the days left and your daily study capacity. Tasks that do not fit are marked high risk.

Which languages does the AI assistant speak?

English and Roman Urdu.
